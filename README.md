### Featured Projects

#### 1. [Agentic AI Data Analyst](https://github.com/RoshanBorkar29/Agentic-AI-Data-Analyst)
> **Problem:** Exploring multi-table CSV datasets with ad-hoc questions typically requires manual SQL/Pandas scripting and iterative debugging.  
> **Approach:** Built a stateful **LangGraph** agent backed by **DuckDB**, **FastAPI**, **Groq LLM**, and **React + Recharts** that translates natural-language queries into validated DuckDB SQL, runs multi-hop investigations, and generates equivalent Pandas code and charts.  
> **Key Engineering:** Implemented SQL guardrails, an automated SQL self-healing loop (up to 2 recovery attempts), SHA-256 query caching, statistical anomaly detection, and **LangSmith** tracing.

#### 2. [Multi-Agent Market Research Platform](https://github.com/RoshanBorkar29/Multi-Agent-Market-Research-Crew)
> **Problem:** Early-stage market validation and competitor analysis require synthesizing fragmented industry, customer, and financial research manually.  
> **Approach:** Engineered **MarketMind AI**, a cyclical **LangGraph `StateGraph`** orchestrating 7 specialized agents (Planner, Market Research, Competitor, Customer, Product Strategy, Evidence Critic, Business Analysis) with **FastAPI**, **React (TypeScript)**, and **pgvector**.  
> **Key Engineering:** Built an Evidence Critic validation loop to check citations and flag data gaps before synthesis, paired with a pgvector RAG assistant ("Ask MarketMind") for querying generated reports.

#### 3. [Financial Document Intelligence](https://github.com/RoshanBorkar29/GenAi-Finance_Analyzer)
> **Problem:** Extracting KPIs, risks, and grounded answers from dense financial PDFs is error-prone when key figures live inside scanned pages, charts, or tables.  
> **Approach:** Developed a **Multimodal RAG** pipeline using **Google Gemini API**, **OCR**, **BM25 + FAISS** hybrid search, and **Streamlit** to analyze annual reports, earnings releases, and investor presentations across text and images.  
> **Key Engineering:** Combined lexical and semantic retrieval with document-level caching to avoid redundant reprocessing and packaged the workflow with **Docker**.
